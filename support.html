Getting started

What do I need to run STAT Recall?

A Mac running macOS 26.1 or later. An iCloud account is optional. Sign in if you want your games to sync between Macs or to share them with other people.

How do I make my first game?

Click Create on the Home screen. A new board starts with six categories, five rows and a $200 base value. Name your categories, click any tile to write its clue and answer, then click Save.

The editor has four sections. Details holds the name, Theme the colors, Board the categories and clues, and Music the songs. The side panel counts how many clues are ready.

How do I start a game?

Click Play on the Home screen, or pick a game in your Library. On Who’s playing?, name two to nine teams or click returning teams to bring them back. Choose Hard Mode under Game rules, then click Start Game or press Return.

Building games

How big can a board be?

Up to 8 categories and 8 rows, for a total of 64 clues. Values are set from the base value: the first row is worth the base value, the second twice that, and so on. If you shrink the board, the app asks before removing a row or column that already has clues.

How do I rearrange the board?

Drag one tile onto another to swap their clues; the point values stay where they are. Use the ‹ › buttons in a category header to move the whole column, or the trash button to remove it.

How do I add a picture, video, sound or YouTube clip?

Open a clue and use Attach File… on the question or answer side to add an image, a video or an audio file. Trim… keeps just part of a clip, or only the audio of a video.

For YouTube, click YouTube… and paste a link. You can set start and end times and preview the clip. YouTube clips stream during the game, so the Mac needs an internet connection then. A few videos can’t play outside YouTube, and the preview shows that right away.

What are Answer Details?

Optional extra background shown under the answer, in smaller text, once it’s revealed. They keep the answer itself short and bold while still giving the explanation.

How do I change a game’s colors and music?

In the editor’s Theme section, choose a solid or gradient background and the tile and text colors from 15 preset colors. The live preview shows the result.

In the Music section, choose the background music that loops while a question is on screen and an optional Game Over song. To use your own songs, open Settings ▸ Music Library ▸ Add Music…. Songs are copied into the app and stay on that Mac.

How do I duplicate or delete a game?

To duplicate the game you last opened, choose Game ▸ Duplicate (⌘D). The copy keeps the clues, values and Double Wagers but not the attached media or theme.

To delete, right-click the game in your Library and choose Delete…. Deleting also removes its saved sessions and can’t be undone.

Playing

How does scoring work?

A correct answer adds the clue’s value. A wrong answer costs nothing, unless Hard Mode is on, in which case it subtracts the value. Scores can go below zero. After every clue the turn passes to the next team.

To give a team the turn, click it on the scoreboard. To fix a score, right-click the team and choose Edit Score.

What is a Double Wager?

Turn on Double Wager for any clue in the editor. When a team picks it, they place a bet before they see the question: from $1 up to their current score, or up to $1,000 if their score is lower. A right answer adds the wager; a wrong answer always subtracts it, even without Hard Mode.

Where is Hard Mode?

On the Who’s playing? screen, in the Game rules card. Your choice is remembered for next time.

How does the question timer work?

Set the length, from 10 to 180 seconds, in Settings. While playing, use the timer button in the toolbar to turn it on or off. If a question has audio or video, the countdown starts once it finishes. When time runs out the music stops and “Time’s up!” appears, and you still mark the answer.

Can I stop a game and finish it later?

Yes. Click the game controller button in the toolbar and choose Save & Exit. Scores, played tiles and whose turn it is are all kept. Resume from the Home screen or from Saved Sessions in your Library.

We ran out of time. Can we still crown a winner?

Yes. Click the game controller button and choose End Early…. The scores stand as they are, the top score wins, and once you click Finish the game counts in Achievements like any finished game. End Early becomes available once at least one clue has been played.

What does Reset Scores do?

Game ▸ Reset Scores sets every score back to zero and clears all played tiles, so the board starts over.

How do I turn the music off?

Click the music button in the play toolbar, or turn off Music in Settings. Both mute everything. The volume slider is in Settings, too.

Teams, groups and achievements

Why does a team name say “used before”?

That name has played before. To keep its record going, pick it from the list of previous teams (the clock button) or click it under Returning teams. If it’s a different team, choose a new name.

How do I change a team’s icon or color?

Open Teams from the Home screen and click the palette button on a team, or right-click it and choose Edit Look…. Pick any emoji, one of the color swatches or a custom color. The team wears that look everywhere, including the next time it plays.

How do I rename a team?

On the Teams screen, click the team’s name and type the new one. The new name appears everywhere, including past games, and its record carries over.

How do I stop tracking a team?

On the Teams screen, hover over the team and click the red minus button, or right-click it and choose Remove Team…. The team leaves the Teams list and Achievements, but the games it played still list it, and other teams’ results stay exactly as they were.

To bring it back, open Removed teams at the bottom of the Teams screen and click Restore. A team that never played and isn’t in a group is deleted instead.

What are groups?

A group is a crowd you play with, such as a class or a weekly team. Its teams come back ready to pick, and Achievements and the Library can show just that group. Use the Playing with switcher to choose a group, create one or manage your groups. A team joins a group automatically when it plays with that group selected.

How are achievements counted?

Achievements come from finished games, recorded when you click Finish on the Game Over screen. Teams are matched by name. A tie counts as a win for every tied team, but it ends a win streak.

There are 17 milestones:

  • Total score: 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, 75,000, 100,000, 500,000 and 1,000,000.
  • Wins: 1, 5, 20, 50 and 100.
  • Win streaks: 3, 5, 10 and 20 wins in a row.
How do I remove a game from my history?

Open Achievements, select the game in the Games list and delete it. This removes it permanently, including from iCloud, and updates every team’s record.

iCloud and sharing

How do I sync STAT Recall between my Macs?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each Mac and make sure iCloud is turned on for STAT Recall. To check, open System Settings, click your name, then iCloud. Games, teams, groups, saved sessions, results and settings sync on their own.

Video files, audio files and songs from your Music Library aren’t synced. They stay on the Mac that added them.

My library looks empty. Where did my games go?

When your Mac isn’t signed in to iCloud, STAT Recall keeps a separate library that stays on that Mac. If you signed out of iCloud, or signed in after making games, you may be looking at the other library. Your games aren’t deleted. Sign back in to the same Apple Account to see your iCloud library again.

How do I share a game?

Right-click the game in your Library and choose Share…. A new game needs to be saved first. Then choose how to share it:

  • Collaborate: one live board. Everyone you add can open it in their Library, and edits merge.
  • Send a copy: they get their own independent game. Later changes on either side stay separate.

Add people by email address or phone number, or copy the invite link and send it yourself. Only the people you invite can join.

Someone sent me a link. How do I open it?

You need STAT Recall installed and your Mac signed in to iCloud. Open the link on your Mac, or copy it and choose Accept a Share Link… in the Library. The game appears under Shared with me.

Why can’t my collaborators see a clue’s video or audio?

Images and YouTube clips are shared with the game. Video and audio files stay on the Mac that added them, so other people see the clue without that clip. Built-in songs carry over, but songs from your own Music Library don’t. For media everyone can play, use an image or a YouTube link.

How do I stop sharing or leave a shared game?

If you own the game, open its sharing window to remove people or choose Stop sharing…. If someone shared it with you, choose Leave…. Copies of games that are no longer shared are kept under “No longer shared”.

What name do collaborators see?

Your display name, shown next to the games you edit. You can change it in the game’s sharing window. If you haven’t set one, STAT Recall uses the full name of your Mac user account.

Your data

Does STAT Recall collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your games stay on your Mac and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete games, saved sessions and finished games from inside the app. To remove STAT Recall’s iCloud data, open System Settings, click your name, then iCloud. Full steps are in the Privacy Policy.
