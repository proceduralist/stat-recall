Trivia night for Mac

Turn any topic into a game show.

Build quiz-show boards, seat up to nine teams and host the whole game from your Mac, with music, a countdown, Double Wagers and a winner’s spotlight. Your teams keep their look, scores and streaks from one game to the next.

Features

Everything a host needs, in one window.

Teaching a class, running a review session or hosting family game night, STAT Recall handles the board, the scoring and the drama so you can focus on the questions.

Build a board in minutes

Up to eight categories and eight rows. Pick a base value and every row is priced for you. Drag one tile onto another to swap clues, or move a whole column with a click.

Clues with pictures, video and sound

Add an image, a video or an audio file to the question or the answer. Trim a clip, keep just its audio for a “name that tune” round, or drop in a YouTube link with start and end times.

Double Wager

Hide a Double Wager behind any clue. The team bets before it sees the question, up to its score or $1,000, whichever is more. Get it right and the wager is theirs; get it wrong and it’s gone.

Hard Mode and a countdown

Turn on Hard Mode and wrong answers cost the clue’s value. Add a 10 to 180 second timer with a countdown ring that waits for any audio or video to finish first.

Up to nine teams

Give each team an emoji and a color. Returning teams come back with their look and record, and about 300 built-in names are ready when you need inspiration.

Your colors, your soundtrack

Style each game from 15 hand-picked colors, solid or gradient. Background music loops while a question is up, and a Game Over song plays for the winners. Add your own songs, too.

Pause, resume or end early

Out of time? Save & Exit keeps every score and played tile so you can pick up later. Or choose End Early to crown the leader on the spot and count the game like any other.

Groups for every crowd

Keep this year’s class, the Tuesday regulars and the family apart. Pick a group and its teams are ready to go, with Achievements and your Library showing just that group.

iCloud sync and sharing

Your games, teams and results follow you to every Mac signed in to your iCloud account. Share a board to build it together, or send someone a copy of their own.

How it works

From blank board to champions.

  1. Build your board

    Name your categories, write each clue and answer, and add Answer Details with the extra background people always ask about.

  2. Pick who’s playing

    Seat two to nine teams. Click a returning team to bring it back, then choose Hard Mode or not before you start.

  3. Host the game

    Click a tile to open the clue, press Space to reveal the answer, and mark it right or wrong. The turn passes to the next team.

  4. Crown the champions

    Confetti, a spotlight and the final standings. Press Finish and the result is recorded, with pop-ups for every milestone a team unlocks.

Achievements

Bragging rights that add up.

Every finished game counts toward each team’s record. See who’s on top of the leaderboard, then filter by date, game or group.

  • Full record for every team: total score, wins, losses, ties, win streaks and win/loss ratio.
  • 17 milestones to chase, from a first win to a million points.
  • Stop tracking a team without rewriting history. Its games still list it, and you can restore it anytime.

Share and collaborate

Write the questions together.

Invite colleagues to a game with iCloud sharing. Everyone sees one live board, and edits merge as you go.

  • Collaborate on one board, or send a copy that becomes their own independent game.
  • Invite-only. Add people by email or phone number, or send them the invite link. Only the people you invite can join.
  • Images and YouTube clips travel with the game. Video and audio files stay on the Mac that added them.

Privacy

Your games stay yours.

Everything you make lives on your Mac and in your own iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.

No accounts

Open the app and start building. Sync uses the iCloud account already on your Mac.

No ads or tracking

No analytics, no advertising and nothing sent to us.

Your iCloud, not ours

Sync runs through your private iCloud database, which we can’t see.

Plays offline

Games run without an internet connection. Only YouTube clips need one.
