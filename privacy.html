Privacy Policy

Your games stay yours.

STAT Recall keeps everything you make on your Mac and in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers that receive your data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Effective October 22, 2026

The short version
  • We, the developer, don't collect, receive, sell or share any of your personal information.
  • Your games, teams and scores are stored on your Mac. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs them through your private iCloud account, which we can't access.
  • When you share a game, the people you invite get that game's content and the display name you've chosen.
  • If a clue includes a YouTube link, the video loads from YouTube, which is run by Google.

Who we are

STAT Recall is a Mac app for building and hosting quiz-show style trivia games. It is published by Proceduralist (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

What the app stores

To work, STAT Recall saves the things you create and the results of the games you play:

  • Games: titles, categories, clues, answers, answer details, point values, Double Wager settings, themes and music choices.
  • Clue media: images you add, YouTube links, and references to the video and audio files you attach.
  • Teams and groups: team names, icons and colors, and the groups you organize them into.
  • Game results: saved games in progress, finished games with their dates, teams and scores, and the achievements calculated from them.
  • Preferences: settings such as Hard Mode, the clue timer, music on or off and music volume.

This information is stored on your Mac. It's never sent to us.

Media files

Video, audio and music files you add are copied into the app's private folder on your Mac. They stay on that Mac. They aren't uploaded to iCloud and aren't sent to anyone you share a game with.

Images are saved inside the game itself, so they sync and share along with it. They're stored exactly as you add them, including any information embedded in the file, such as the location where a photo was taken. If you don't want that information to travel with a game, remove it from the image before you add it.

iCloud sync

If your Mac is signed in to iCloud, STAT Recall uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your games, teams, groups, saved games, results and preferences in sync across your Macs. This data is kept in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. We can't see it.

iCloud is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your Mac only.

Sharing and collaboration

You can invite people to play or edit one of your games using iCloud sharing. Shares are invite-only: nobody can open a game unless you add them or send them the link.

What people you invite receive

  • The shared game's content: its title, categories, clues, answers, answer details, images, YouTube links, theme, music choices and default team names.
  • The display name you set in the app, shown as the person who last edited the game. If you haven't set one, the app uses the full name of your Mac user account. You can change it at any time in the game's sharing window.

What stays private

  • Your video and audio files. Collaborators see only that a clue has one; they'd need to add their own copy.
  • Your game history, scores, achievements, saved games, teams and groups.

Information about other people

To invite someone, you enter their email address or phone number, and Apple uses it to find their iCloud account. The app shows each participant's name, or their email or phone number if no name is available, as provided by iCloud. When someone shares a game with you, their name is shown and saved with your copy of the game. All of this is handled by Apple's iCloud and isn't sent to us.

YouTube clips

A clue can include a YouTube video. When you open such a clue, or add a YouTube link in the editor, the app loads YouTube's embedded player and the video's thumbnail directly from YouTube's servers. YouTube, a Google service, may receive information such as your IP address and may store cookies inside the app, as it does on any website with an embedded video. That is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. Games without YouTube links never contact YouTube.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, usage data or diagnostics, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • The app has no advertising and doesn't track you across apps or websites.
  • There are no accounts to create and no in-app purchases.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • The app's activity log stays on your Mac and is never sent to us.

Permissions

STAT Recall doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos library or location. It reads only the files you choose with the standard macOS file picker. It uses the internet only for iCloud and for YouTube clips.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete in the app: delete games, saved games and finished games in your history from inside the app. On a Mac using iCloud, deletions sync to your other Macs. Removing a team on the Teams screen stops tracking it, but its name stays in the games it played until you delete those games.
  • Stop sharing: stop sharing a game or remove people from the game's sharing window. If someone shared a game with you, you can leave it there too.
  • Turn off iCloud sync: open System Settings, click your name, then iCloud, and turn off STAT Recall. You can also review and delete its iCloud data from there.
  • Remove everything from a Mac: delete the app and its data folder, ~/Library/Containers/com.trivia.statrecall.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question about your data, we're happy to help.

Children

STAT Recall isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
